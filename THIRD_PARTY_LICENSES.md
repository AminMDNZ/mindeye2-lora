# Third-party components

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers the code in this repository. It does **not** cover
what the pipeline downloads and imports at runtime. This file lists those components and
their terms.

Two of them matter more than the rest:

- **The Natural Scenes Dataset requires you to accept its terms before use**, and those
  terms restrict redistribution and require citation. This is a condition, not a courtesy.
- **The SDXL unCLIP decoder is not MIT.** It is derived from Stable Diffusion XL, released
  under the CreativeML Open RAIL++-M licence, which carries use restrictions that MIT does
  not.

Licences change. The table records what was true when this was written; verify at the
source before relying on it, particularly for anything commercial.

## Data

| component | source | terms |
|---|---|---|
| Natural Scenes Dataset (NSD) | [naturalscenesdataset.org](https://naturalscenesdataset.org) | [NSD terms and conditions](https://cvnlab.slite.page/p/IB6BSeW_7o/Terms-and-Conditions) — acceptance required before download; restricts redistribution; requires citation of Allen et al. (2022) |

The preprocessed HuggingFace repo is a *derivative of NSD*. Downloading it does not
exempt you from the NSD conditions.

## What this repository does and does not redistribute

This repository **does not** redistribute model weights, fMRI data, or vendored copies of upstream code.
Everything is fetched at runtime from its original source:

- `upstream.py` clones MindEyeV2 and imports its modules rather than vendoring a copy,
  which keeps attribution unambiguous and guarantees weight compatibility.
- `assets.py` downloads data and checkpoints from HuggingFace into a local workspace that
  is `.gitignore`d.

The figures in `docs/figures/` are outputs generated by this pipeline. They depict COCO
stimulus images and reconstructions derived from them, so the underlying images carry
COCO's terms.
